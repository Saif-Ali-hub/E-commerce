🚚 Logistics & Delivery

Shipping & Delivery Information

Transparent fulfillment timelines, express logistics partners, and delivery standards.

🇮🇳

Pan-India Delivery Availability

We dispatch across all serviceable pincodes in India via vetted tier-1 courier networks. Both residential addresses and corporate work hubs are supported.

⏱️

Estimated Delivery Timeframe

Metropolitan Hubs: 2 to 4 business days from dispatch.
Regional & Outstation Locations: 4 to 7 business days depending on local postal accessibility.

⚡

Same-Day Order Processing

Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM IST on business days undergo quality inspection, protective packaging, and carrier handover on the exact same day.

📦

Real-Time Delivery Tracking

Every confirmed order receives a unique 24-character Order ID. Track status milestones anytime via the Order Tracking tool or your My Orders dashboard.

💰

Zero Delivery Fees > ₹499

Free express shipping applies automatically to all cart orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 incur a nominal fulfillment charge calculated during checkout.

🛡️

Tamper-Proof Packaging

All products are sealed in protective, tamper-evident security cartons. Please verify the exterior seal before accepting delivery from our courier representative.

🔄 Customer Assurance

7-Day Easy Returns & Refunds

Hassle-free return window, transparent eligibility guidelines, and prompt refund processing.

📅

7-Day Return Window

You have 7 full calendar days from the doorstep delivery timestamp to initiate a return request if an item is damaged, defective, or not as described.

✅

Basic Return Eligibility

Items must remain unused, unwashed, and in original condition with all barcode tags, user manuals, accessories, and protective packaging intact.

📝

Return Request Process

1. Open your order in My Orders.
2. Note your 24-character Order ID.
3. Submit a return inquiry stating the reason. Our logistics partner schedules a scheduled doorstep pickup.

💳

Refund Processing Timelines

Prepaid (UPI / Net Banking): Returned to original source within 3–5 business days after warehouse inspection.
Cash on Delivery (COD): Refunded via verified UPI or bank transfer upon item handover.

⚠️

Important Exceptions & Conditions

For hygiene and safety reasons, personal grooming items, custom altered items, or products showing signs of customer misuse/wear are non-returnable.

🤝

Free Reverse Pickup

Approved return requests enjoy free doorstep courier pickup across major serviceable locations with no return shipping surcharge.

How MiniStore collects, utilizes, and safeguards your customer information.

Information We Collect

We collect only necessary personal data: account name, email address, delivery addresses, telephone numbers, and order histories entered during registration and checkout.

🔐 Security Architecture

Security Architecture & Verification

How your transactions, customer accounts, and sessions are safely safeguarded.

🔑

Cryptographic Password Hashing

All customer passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using multi-round bcrypt prior to persistence. Plaintext passwords are never recorded or stored in our database.

🛡️

JWT Stateless Authentication

Account sessions are secured using cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Every protected API request validates token authenticity and expiry.

👤

Strict Account Isolation

Robust authorization policies ensure customers can strictly view and modify only their own cart, orders, and delivery addresses, preventing cross-account access.

💳

Safe Transaction Workflows

Transactions are processed via validated UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery workflows. MiniStore stores zero sensitive debit/credit card numbers on its servers.

🔍

Input Sanitization & Protection

Backend routes enforce strict MongoDB ObjectId validation, payload schema checks, and query protections against unauthorized injections.

ℹ️

Transparent Engineering Disclosure

This overview reflects the actual technical safeguards engineered into MiniStore. We operate on honest technical merit without claiming unverified third-party certifications.
